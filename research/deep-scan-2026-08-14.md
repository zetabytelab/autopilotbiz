### Pattern worth flagging
A cluster of **AI-native professional-services firms** (Moritz, LegalOS, Keith, arguably Accrual) is emerging in mid-2026: structured as the actual licensed service provider, with agents doing the substantive billable work under thin professional review. This is more defensible than generic "agent tooling" since the agents perform the firm's *own* core operations. Worth a dedicated follow-up pass once more of these clear 6–12 months of operating history and disclose revenue.

## 2. Field experiments (the Guinndex rule)

Two new experiments found with real numbers; a third is an expansion of an already-tracked project, flagged separately. Several other leads (Döner-Preis-Index, a "Big Baguette Index" writeup, a human-run Glasgow "Flat White Index") were excluded — either non-AI surveys or restatements of Le Baguette Index.

### SliceDex — NYC pizza slice price index
- **Who:** A builder going by "JP," described as an NYU Stern student, explicitly inspired by Guinndex.
- **What the agent did:** Voice agent "Heather" (Twilio + ElevenLabs) phoned pizza shops across all five NYC boroughs asking the price of a plain cheese slice; Claude parsed transcripts into structured price data for a live, daily-updating index.
- **Scale:** 1,766 shops called, 1,346 answered, 838 gave a price (~829 in the live index after outlier exclusion).
- **Findings:** Avg ~$3.42/slice (~$3.56 excluding $1–1.50 dollar-slice outliers) — roughly consistent with independent non-AI tracking (Liam Quigley's 8-year manual pizza log, covered by Slate/FlowingData/PMQ Pizza).
- **Verification:** **Self-reported only** — no dedicated press coverage found in this pass; worth a follow-up check.
- **Sources:** [slicedex.com](https://slicedex.com)

### Flat White Index — AI agent "Mia" calls independent cafés (Sydney, London)
- **Who:** Builder unconfirmed; the London edition runs under a brand called "TheDX." Unclear whether Sydney/London/global editions are one team or independent copycats of the format.
- **What the agent did:** Voice agent "Mia" calls independent (non-chain) coffee shops asking the price of a regular flat white; prices extracted from transcripts into an open dataset.
- **Scale:** Sydney — 847 cafés called, mapped by suburb, avg A$5.80 (range A$4.00–A$7.20), published as open CC BY 4.0 JSON. London — 2,288 independent shops called across all 33 boroughs, 416 prices captured.
- **Tech stack:** Not confirmed beyond "AI voice agent" — no named telephony/LLM vendor found (unlike Guinndex/SliceDex).
- **Verification:** **Self-reported only** — no independent press found (checked TechCrunch, Cybernews, news.com.au). Note: a separate, unrelated human-curated "Flat White Index" exists in Glasgow (theflatwhiteindex.com) — do not conflate.
- **Sources:** [flatwhiteindex.com.au](https://flatwhiteindex.com.au) · [flatwhiteindex.thedx.ai](https://flatwhiteindex.thedx.ai) · [flatwhiteindex.com](https://flatwhiteindex.com)

### Flagged, not counted as new — Guinndex UK
Matt Cortland (Guinndex's original builder) and a collaborator ("John") reportedly extended the project to the UK, indexing 46,235+ pubs via Google Maps API (June 2026, per Inside the Cask).
