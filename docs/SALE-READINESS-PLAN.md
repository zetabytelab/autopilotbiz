# Sale-readiness plan

## Objective

Build Autopilot Index as a transferable research, audience and data business that can be acquired without the buyer needing the founder to publish every day.

The public site remains free. The sale case comes from the assets around it: an owned audience, recurring revenue, proprietary historical data, trusted editorial processes and a documented operating system.

## Current baseline (1 September 2026)

- 4 owned email subscribers.
- 811 LinkedIn newsletter subscribers and 18,900 LinkedIn followers.
- Approximately 2.5 owner-hours per day.
- 28 company profiles and a public read-only API/MCP server.
- Free public access; monetization is currently affiliate links and optional reader support.

LinkedIn reach is distribution, not an owned asset. Email, recurring revenue and reusable data are the priority measures for sale readiness.

## Next 90 days

### Audience

- Add a clear email value proposition to every high-intent page.
- Track confirmation, source, open rate, click rate and 30-day retention.
- Reach 100 confirmed subscribers, then 500; do not count unconfirmed requests.

### Revenue

- Test one paid offer: a weekly operator brief, company-monitoring alerts, or API/data access.
- Track monthly recurring revenue, gross margin, churn and revenue concentration.
- Keep the free index as the acquisition channel and document conversion to the paid offer.

### Proprietary data

- Store dated observations instead of overwriting current values.
- Give every financial observation a metric type, period, currency, company scope, source, confidence and review date.
- Give every autonomy assessment an evidence type, observation window, human-intervention measure and reviewer note.

### Transferability

- Write the research, correction, newsletter and release runbooks.
- Keep credentials, domains, partner terms, analytics ownership and content rights in a transfer inventory.
- Measure founder hours by activity and reduce work requiring founder-only judgment.

## Monthly dashboard

Report owned subscribers, confirmed growth, open/click rates, recurring revenue, gross margin, churn, organic sessions, returning users, API usage, content output, correction rate, owner-hours and revenue concentration. Follower counts and page views are supporting signals, not substitutes for these measures.

## Buyer narrative

Autopilot Index is a public research brand and structured intelligence product tracking AI-operated businesses. Its defensibility is the combination of evidence review, historical company and autonomy data, an owned audience and a repeatable agent-assisted publishing system. This narrative becomes credible as audience, revenue and transferability metrics accumulate.
