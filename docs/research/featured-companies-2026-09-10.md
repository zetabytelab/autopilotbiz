## Method and limits

- Reviewed public company product, pricing and help/terms pages, plus vendor-published customer accounts. Sources below are first-party publications; their customer claims remain attributed claims.
- No paid account, checkout, payment ledger, customer interview or production workflow was independently tested in this research pass.
- Dynamic counters are dated observations. Marketing demos, generic industry statistics, funding and acquisitions are not evidence of autonomous customer-business profits.
- Polsia's direct page extraction returned an empty body. Its September 7 policy was available as a full indexed first-party search result. This access limitation is visible on its profile.
- Missing evidence means it was not established by the cited pages reviewed, not that no evidence could exist elsewhere.
- Historical registry figures remain in `lib/data.ts`; refreshed profile product and pricing sections take precedence over older descriptions. The source list includes both current research and historical references.
