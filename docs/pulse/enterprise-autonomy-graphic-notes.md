## Alt text

Autopilot Index infographic titled “Paths towards autonomous business functions.” An editorial ladder progresses from L0 manual operation through L1 assistance, L2 supervised execution and L3 conditional operation to L4 bounded autonomy. L4 means operation and fallback within defined limits; L3 requires a human available to take over. L5 general autonomy is labelled aspirational. A separate, unranked examples panel lists Resolve AI and NeuBird for SRE and production operations, Basis for accounting, Minimal for customer support, and Atoms and NanoCorp for app and business creation. The takeaway is “Assess the workflow, not the logo.” The framework is an analogy, not an SAE standard or certification, inspired by Augustin Friedel’s automotive map.

## Caption qualification

These proposed levels describe the human/system relationship in a defined workflow. They are separate from the index's existing company-level labels. Vendor names are examples by function, not assigned autonomy ratings. A safe stop is not a successfully completed task; completion and safe containment must be measured separately. Human organisational accountability remains.

Sources and the accompanying post are in `enterprise-autonomy-post.md`. No website deployment or social posting is part of creating this graphic.
