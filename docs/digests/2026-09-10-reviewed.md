# What it costs to delegate

## The signal

The useful question for a solo founder is how much work a plan lets you delegate, and what happens when its trial or credits end. This review turns up five practical differences to check before choosing a stack. None of these product descriptions establishes that a customer's business operates profitably without supervision.

### 1. Cofounder's listed Pro plan starts at $120 a month

The pricing page lists Pro from $120/month and a seven-day trial. Team is shown at $100 per seat but marked coming soon. External spending and statutory fees remain separate. This is materially different from the older $20 price in our research record; we have not established when the change occurred.

**Implication:** budget for the current advertised plan and its external costs before comparing the economics of a Cofounder-built business. A forthcoming tier is not an available purchase option.

[Primary source: Cofounder pricing](https://cofounder.co/pricing)

### 2. NanoCorp's free access is a short trial, not continuing operations

The pricing page describes a three-day, no-card trial with 15 welcome credits. It says checkout, agents, email and custom domains pause after the trial unless a plan is selected. The Founder plan is advertised at $30/month with 30 credits and a 20% withdrawal fee.

**Implication:** a generated site remaining visible does not establish that its business processes remain active. Include the trial cutoff and withdrawal terms in any launch test.

[Primary source: NanoCorp pricing](https://www.nanocorp.so/pricing)

### 3. Lindy's approval rules matter as much as its seat price

Lindy lists Plus at $29.99/user/month with 3,000 credits. Its pricing FAQ says externally impactful actions wait for approval, while read-only work can proceed. The seven-day trial described for Slack joiners differs from immediate billing for direct signups.

**Implication:** measure the approval workload in the workflow you intend to delegate. Check the signup route before assuming that a trial applies.

[Primary source: Lindy pricing and FAQ](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)

### 4. Sauna makes the amount of delegated authority configurable

Sauna lists Free, Lite ($29), Basic ($99), Pro ($299) and Team ($999) monthly plans. Its pricing material describes autonomy ranging from suggestions to independent execution. Free credit allowances have conditions, including a higher allowance with a verified card; annual billing is not offered on the reviewed page.

**Implication:** compare the plan, permissions and actual mode used in an experiment. A product that offers independent execution may still be running a particular task in suggestion mode.

[Primary source: Sauna pricing](https://www.sauna.ai/pricing)

### 5. Caffeine separates building access from hosting

Caffeine displays Business at $100/month for two seats, Studio at $10 for the first month then $25, and Host at $5. The free tier is presented with welcome credits and restricted building access. The reviewed cards and comparison table were not fully consistent about free features; an annual-discount option also makes the selected billing period important.

**Implication:** verify both the ability to build and the cost of keeping the result running. Record the billing period and account entitlements instead of treating a single headline price as the whole cost.

[Primary source: Caffeine pricing](https://caffeine.ai/pricing)
