# Short commentary options

Drafts for individual posts or comments. Choose one for the relevant context.

## 1. The missing metric

The metric I want beside revenue in an AI business: human minutes per accepted result. Count review, corrections, support and the founder rescuing the workflow. Then see whether that number falls as demand grows.

## 2. A practical founder test

Pick one agent workflow. Follow ten jobs from request to accepted result. Record every human intervention and why it happened. That gives you a concrete starting point for deciding what to automate next.

## 3. Measuring the outcome

An agent completing more tasks is promising. I would also track the share accepted, the cost per result and the human time needed to get there. Those numbers tell me whether the operation is improving.
